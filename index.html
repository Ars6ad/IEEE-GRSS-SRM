About IEEE GRSS SRM

Inspiring students and researchers to utilize Earth observation data, spatial analytics, and satellite remote sensing for sustainable development.

Our Vision & Mission

The IEEE Geoscience and Remote Sensing Society (GRSS) is a global professional organization dedicated to advancing the science and technology of remote sensing of the Earth, oceans, atmosphere, and space.

At SRM IST Chapter, our mission is to cultivate a collaborative ecosystem where passionate students, coders, and researchers gain hands-on expertise in satellite image analysis, GIS mapping, AI models for Earth observation, and drone remote sensing.

Satellite Remote Sensing

Processing Sentinel-2, Landsat, and MODIS satellite data stream for environmental monitoring.

AI & GIS Analytics

Applying Computer Vision & Deep Learning to land-cover change, crop yield, and wildfire tracking.

Hands-on Workshops

Technical bootcamps in Python, QGIS, Google Earth Engine (GEE), and GDAL spatial tools.

Hackathons & Research

Participating in international IEEE challenges, hackathons, and publishing high-impact papers.
